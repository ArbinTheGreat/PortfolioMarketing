How I work

A weekly loop, not a one-time plan

STEP 1

Audit

Technical site audit and keyword-gap analysis first, so the content plan answers real demand instead of guesses.

STEP 2

Listen

Discord, Telegram, Reddit, forums, comments. Community sentiment is the earliest signal a product or policy has a problem.

STEP 3

Publish

Short-form educational video, pain-point posts, SEO value posts, UGC-style collabs, and conversion-focused captions.

STEP 4

Measure

Weekly review of watch time, saves and shares, profile visits, story link clicks, and qualified versus warm leads.

STEP 5

Decide

Scale what converts, refine what nearly works, retire what does not, then pick the next test.

STEP 6

Systemize

Playbooks, briefs, bots, and dashboards so the result survives after I move to the next problem.
